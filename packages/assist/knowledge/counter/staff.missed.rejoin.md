# Missed tickets

Missed tickets appear under **On hold and missed** in the console.

- Press **Return to queue** to put a missed visitor back in line. This works only inside the service's grace window, counted from when the ticket was marked missed. After that, the visitor joins again.
- This needs a connection.

## The service rule

- **Staff decide** (the default): only staff return missed visitors.
- **Visitor may return themselves:** the visitor can also do it from their ticket page inside the window, except while an offline coordinator holds the queue.

## Who can do this

- Return a missed visitor to the queue: Reception, Counter operator, Supervisor, Organization administrator.
