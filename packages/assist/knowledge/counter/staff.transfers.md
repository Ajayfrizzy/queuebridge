# To another service

While serving a visitor, use **Transfer this ticket**. Choose the service and a reason, then press **Transfer**. The visitor gets a new ticket code. Depending on the service rules, they keep their original waiting time or join the end of the queue.

# To another counter

Use **Send to another counter** for a visitor who is called or being served, with a reason. This needs a connection.

## History

The original ticket and the transfer stay linked in the audit trail.

## Who can do this

- Transfer to another service: Counter operator, Supervisor, Organization administrator.
- Send to another counter: Counter operator, Supervisor, Organization administrator.
