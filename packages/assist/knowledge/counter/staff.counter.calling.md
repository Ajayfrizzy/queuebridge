# At the counter

1. Choose your counter and open it if needed.
2. Press **Call next**. The next visitor in the published order is called to your counter.
3. When they arrive, press **Start serving**. When you finish, press **Complete**.
4. If they do not come, press **Did not come (missed)**.

- **Put on hold** pauses a visitor you are serving. **Return to queue** puts a held visitor back in line.
- **Return to queue (recall)** moves a called visitor back to waiting.

## Closing your counter

Choose a reason and close it. A counter cannot be closed while it is serving a visitor. Closing it while a visitor is called returns them to the front of the line; only supervisors can do that.

## Who can do this

- Call next: Counter operator, Supervisor, Organization administrator.
- Start serving: Counter operator, Supervisor, Organization administrator.
- Complete: Counter operator, Supervisor, Organization administrator.
- Did not come (missed): Counter operator, Supervisor, Organization administrator.
- Put on hold: Counter operator, Supervisor, Organization administrator.
- Return a held visitor to the queue: Counter operator, Supervisor, Organization administrator.
- Recall a called visitor: Supervisor, Organization administrator.

Other roles see the console without calling controls.
