# Text message alerts (test mode)

Text messages are in test mode here (sandbox): no real text messages are sent. The steps below show how it will work.

## Asking for a text message

- When you get a ticket, you can enter your **Mobile number** and tick the box to get a text message when you are called.
- Both are optional. You can join without them.
- Your number is used only for this ticket. It is stored encrypted and deleted when your visit ends.

## Stopping text messages

- On your ticket page, under **Text message alert**, press **Stop text messages**. Your number is deleted at once.
- A member of staff can also stop them for you.
- Where the service supports replies, replying STOP stops them too. A reply cannot always be matched to one service: if messages keep coming, use **Stop text messages** on your ticket page or ask a member of staff.

## When the text message is sent

- Usually as soon as you are called.
- Some facilities wait a short time first. If your ticket page has already shown that you are called, no text message is sent.

## Rely on your ticket page

A text message can arrive late or not at all. Your ticket page, the public board and staff calling your ticket show your turn.
