# Sign in

1. Open [sign-in](route:public.login).
2. Enter your organization's URL identifier (slug), your email and your password.
3. You arrive at administration. The staff console is in the top menu.

## If you cannot sign in

- The message is the same whatever was wrong, so check all three fields.
- There is no password reset in the app. Tell your organization administrator.
- A deactivated account cannot sign in.

## Sign out

- Press **Sign out** at the top of the page.
- If this device holds unsent offline actions, you are warned first. Send them, or sign out and discard them knowingly.
- A session ends by itself after 12 hours.
