# Save your recovery key

- When you get a ticket, the page shows your recovery key once. Save it straight away.
- You can copy it, save it as a file, print it, share it, or show it as a QR code to scan with another phone.
- The page does not keep the key. After a reload it is gone, but you can make a new one.
- Keep it private. Anyone with the key can open your ticket.
- The ticket code on the board is not enough to get your ticket back.

## Make a new key

Press **Get a new recovery key** on your ticket page. The old key stops working.

## Sign out other devices

- If someone else may have your key, press **Sign out other devices**. Other devices lose access and you get a new key.
- If the page says access was given to another device and it was not you, do the same.
- At most three devices can have your ticket open. Opening it on another device signs out the one that got access first.

## Good to know

- Recovering, or making a new key, does not change your place in the queue.
- If the page says self-service recovery is not available here, ask staff if you lose this device.
