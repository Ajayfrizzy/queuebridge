# Ticket statuses

- **Waiting:** you are in the queue. The page shows how many tickets are ahead of you.
- **Called:** it is your turn. Go to the counter shown on the page.
- **Being served:** you are at the counter.
- **On hold:** please speak to a member of staff.
- **Missed:** you were called but were not there. See the help on missing your turn.
- **Completed:** your visit is finished.
- **Cancelled:** the ticket was cancelled. Speak to staff if this is unexpected.
- **Transferred:** staff moved you to another service with a new ticket code. The page shows your original join time.

## Confirmed by the server

The page shows **Confirmed by the server** for information the server has confirmed. Offline, it shows the last confirmed information and says so.

## Who can see your ticket

- Ticket status is private. Only a device that received the ticket, or one you recovered it on, can open it.
- If the page says this device has no access or the ticket has expired, use your recovery key or ask staff.
