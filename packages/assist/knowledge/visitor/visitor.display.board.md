# What the board shows

- **Now calling:** ticket codes and the counter to go to.
- **Next:** the next ticket codes in line.
- How many people are waiting for each service, and whether a queue is paused.

## Privacy

The board shows ticket codes and counter names only. It never shows names, phone numbers, priority classes or reasons.

## Spoken announcements

Tick **Spoken announcements** on the board to have new calls read aloud by the browser. It is off until someone turns it on.

## For staff

The facility overview links to the board, next to the join link and QR code. Use it on a screen in the waiting area.
