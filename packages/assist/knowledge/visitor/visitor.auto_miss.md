# Automatic missed turns

- Some services mark a called ticket as missed if nobody responds within a set time. This is off unless the facility turns it on.
- When it applies, your ticket page states the rule, in seconds, while you are being called.
- The time counts from when you were called, by the server's clock.
- It does not depend on whether an alert reached your phone.
- It never runs while the desk is working offline.

After a missed turn, see the help on missing your turn.
