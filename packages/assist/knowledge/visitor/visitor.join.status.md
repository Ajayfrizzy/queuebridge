# Join the queue

1. Scan the QR code at the facility, or open the join link you were given.
2. Choose your service and press **Get a ticket**.
3. Your ticket page opens. You do not need an account, a name or a phone number.

## Your ticket page

- It shows your ticket code, your status and, while you wait, your position in line.
- It shows an estimated wait. This is a range, not an appointment.
- The ticket is saved on this device. Open it again from **Your tickets on this device** on the join page or the home page.

## Good to know

- If this device already has a ticket here, tick the box for someone else (for example a family member) to get a second one.
- If the page says no queue is open, or the queue is paused, ask a member of staff.
- If getting a ticket fails because of the connection, retrying is safe. It does not create a second ticket.
- No smartphone? Ask staff for a walk-in ticket and watch for your code on the [public display board](route:public.display).
