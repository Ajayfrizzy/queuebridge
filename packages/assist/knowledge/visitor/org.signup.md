# Create your organization

1. Open [organization sign-up](route:public.signup), or press **Set up your facility** on the home page.
2. Enter the organization name and its URL identifier (slug). The slug appears in join links and is needed when staff sign in.
3. Enter your name, your email and a password of at least 12 characters.
4. Press **Create organization**. You become the organization administrator and are signed in.

## Next steps

- Create a facility in **Administration**, which opens after you sign up.
- Add services and counters, and open today's queue before you share the join link.
