# Push alerts (optional)

- If this site has push alerts set up, your ticket page offers **Get a push alert (optional)**. Your browser asks for permission only after you press it.
- A push alert can reach you while the page is in the background. It is best effort: it may arrive late or not at all.
- On iPhone and iPad, push alerts work only in the app added to the Home Screen.
- If notifications are blocked, allow them in your browser's site settings and reload the page.
- Press **Turn off push alert** to stop it for this ticket on this device.

## Always

Your ticket page, the public board and staff calling still show your turn, with or without push.
