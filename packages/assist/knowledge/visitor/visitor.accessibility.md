# Display and alerts

Press **Display and alerts** at the top of any page.

- **Contrast:** follow your device setting, or high contrast.
- **Text size:** normal, large or extra large.
- **Motion:** follow your device setting, or reduce motion to remove animation.
- **Alerts:** quiet mode, sound, vibration and a test alert.
- **Reset to defaults** puts everything back.

## Stored on this device

The settings stay in this browser on this device. They are not sent anywhere, they work without a connection and they do not follow you to another device.
