# Ask a member of staff

- Staff will ask you some questions about your visit before they show you anything about the ticket.
- A second member of staff may need to approve.
- You finish on your own phone by scanning a code that staff show you. Staff never see or read out your key.
- Some facilities do not offer this. Staff can still help you join the queue again.
