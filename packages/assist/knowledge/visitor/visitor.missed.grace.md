# What missed means

Your ticket was called, you did not reach the counter, and it was marked as missed.

## The grace window

- Each service has a grace window, counted from when the ticket was marked missed. It is 5 minutes unless the facility changed it. The queue rules on your ticket page show it in seconds.
- Inside the window, staff can return a missed ticket to the queue.

## Returning yourself

- Some facilities let you return yourself. Your ticket page then shows **I am here, return me to the queue** and the time it is available until.
- It is not offered while the desk is working offline.
- If you do not see that button, ask a member of staff, or join the queue again.
- After the window has passed, join the queue again.

## Alerts do not decide

Whether an alert reached you never decides if you are marked missed.
