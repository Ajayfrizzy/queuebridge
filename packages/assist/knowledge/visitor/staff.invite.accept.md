# Accept an invitation

1. Open the invitation link your administrator gave you.
2. Check the organization, facility, role and sign-in email shown.
3. Enter your name and a password of at least 12 characters. If you already have an account in this organization, enter your existing password.
4. Press **Accept invitation**. You are signed in.

## If the link does not work

An invitation link works once and expires after 7 days. Ask your administrator for a new one.
