# Recover with your recovery key

1. Open [ticket recovery](route:visitor.recover).
2. Open the recovery link you saved or scan its code, or type the organization, facility, ticket code and recovery key.
3. Press **Recover my ticket**. Your ticket opens on this phone. Your place in the queue does not change.

Spaces, hyphens and capital letters in the key do not matter.

## If it does not work

- The ticket code on the board is not enough on its own. You need the recovery key.
- After several wrong tries, wait up to 15 minutes and try again. You are never locked out for good.
- Recovery needs a connection.
- Only an active ticket can be recovered. A completed or cancelled ticket cannot.
- A temporary ticket made while the service was offline can be recovered only after it is confirmed.
