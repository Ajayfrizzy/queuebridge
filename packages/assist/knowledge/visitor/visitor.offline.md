# When you are offline

- Your ticket page shows the last information the server confirmed, with the time, and says it may be out of date.
- Nothing new is confirmed until you are back online. The page updates by itself when the connection returns.
- Alerts may be delayed. Watch the public board and listen for staff calling your ticket.
- Recovering a ticket needs a connection.
- Display settings work offline.
