# When you are called

Your ticket page shows **It is your turn** with your ticket code and counter, and announces it to screen readers. This needs no permission.

## Sound and vibration

- **Play a short sound** is off until you turn it on. It plays once per call and never repeats.
- **Vibrate** is on where your device supports it. It works only while the ticket page is open and on screen.
- **Quiet mode** turns off sound and vibration. The message on the page stays.
- **Test alert** tries the cues you have switched on.
- Find these under **Display and alerts**.

## Rely on the board too

Sound and vibration cannot work while the page is closed. Alerts are best effort. The public board and staff calling your ticket are the reliable way to know it is your turn.
