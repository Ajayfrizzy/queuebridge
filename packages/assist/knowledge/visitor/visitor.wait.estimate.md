# Estimated wait

- The estimate is a range, for example about 10 to 20 minutes. It is never an exact time.
- It uses the tickets ahead, how many counters are open for the service and how long recent visits took.
- A label shows how much recent history it is based on: higher, medium or low confidence.
- With too little history, or no open counter, the page says there is not enough information for an estimate yet.
- It changes as people are served, as counters open or close, and while calling is paused.
- Open **How this is estimated** to see the reasons.

## An estimate, not an appointment

The actual wait can be shorter or longer. Stay nearby and watch your ticket page or the public board.
