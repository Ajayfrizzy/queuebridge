# Counters

Open the facility in administration and choose **Counters**. Facility administrators and organization administrators can change them.

1. Under **Add a counter**, enter a name and press **Add counter**.
2. Tick the services the counter serves. A counter with no active service is not operational.

## Deactivating a counter

- A counter that is serving a visitor cannot be deactivated.
- A visitor already called to it goes back to the front of the queue.
- Its service ticks are kept for when it is activated again.

## Day to day

Counter operators and supervisors open and close their counter in the queue console.
