# Invite someone

1. Open [staff management](route:admin.facility.staff).
2. Enter their email address, choose a role and press **Create invitation**.
3. Copy the invitation link and give it to them yourself. No email is sent. The link is shown once and expires after 7 days.

You can revoke a pending invitation. To send it again, create a new one.

## Roles you can give

Reception, Counter operator, Supervisor, Facility administrator and Auditor (read-only). An organization administrator is created only by organization sign-up.

## Change roles

- Add a role with **Add role**, or remove one with the button on its chip.
- A facility must keep at least one facility administrator.

## Deactivate an account

Only an organization administrator can deactivate or reactivate an account. Deactivating ends the person's sessions at once.
