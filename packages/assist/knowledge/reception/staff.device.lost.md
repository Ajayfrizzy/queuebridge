# If a device is lost

Tell a supervisor straight away.

## For supervisors and facility administrators

1. Open [connectivity and recovery](route:staff.connectivity).
2. Press **Revoke** on the device, choose a reason such as lost or stolen, and confirm.

- Its unsent actions can never be sent and may be lost.
- If it was the coordinator, its authority ends and its offline actions go to review.
- Revoking does not sign the person out. Deactivate or sign out the user if needed.
- If the device is found, it stays revoked. Register it again as a new device.
