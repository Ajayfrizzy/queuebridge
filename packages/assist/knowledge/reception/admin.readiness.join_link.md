# Is the facility ready?

The facility **Overview** shows **Configuration readiness**. It is ready when at least one active counter serves at least one active service. Having services and counters is not enough on its own.

## The join link and QR code

- **Live** means visitors can join now. That needs a ready configuration and a queue opened today.
- **Not live yet** means do not share or print the code for visitors yet. The QR shown is only a preview.
- Use **Copy link**, **Download QR (SVG)** or **Print**.

## Opening today's queue

A supervisor, facility administrator or organization administrator presses **Open today's queue** in the facility's queue list.

## Public board

The overview also links to the public display board for a screen in the waiting area.
