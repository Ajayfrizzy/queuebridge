# Staff roles

- **Reception:** views queues, issues walk-in tickets, helps with ticket recovery, cancels waiting tickets and returns missed tickets inside the grace window.
- **Counter operator:** reception's tasks, plus calling, serving and completing visitors, marking missed, putting on hold and transferring.
- **Supervisor:** counter work, plus opening and closing queues, priority changes, calling out of order, approving staff-assisted recovery, the audit trail, connectivity and recovery, coordinators and conflict review.
- **Facility administrator:** sets up services, counters and staff, opens and closes queues, changes priority, approves recovery, and reads the audit trail and connectivity. Does not call or serve visitors.
- **Organization administrator:** everything in every facility of the organization, plus creating facilities and deactivating accounts.
- **Auditor (read-only):** reads facility settings, queues, the audit trail and connectivity. Cannot change anything.

If a screen or button is missing, your role does not allow it. Ask your facility administrator.
