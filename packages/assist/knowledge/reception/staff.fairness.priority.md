# The published order

Visitors are called by priority class, where the service has classes, then by how long they have waited. The console shows the service's rules.

## Changing a visitor's priority

1. Open **Supervisor actions** and use the **Change priority** form. **Close without saving** discards the draft and keeps the ticket in the queue.
2. Pick a reason from the service's list and write an explanation of at least 5 characters.
3. Confirm. The change is recorded with your name, the reason and the policy version.

## Privacy

The explanation is private and shown only in the audit trail. The public board shows ticket codes only, never classes or reasons.

## Who can do this

- Change priority: Supervisor, Facility administrator, Organization administrator.
