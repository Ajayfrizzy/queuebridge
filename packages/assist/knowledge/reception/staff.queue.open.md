# Today's queues

The facility's queue list shows each service as **Open**, **Paused**, **Closed** or **Not opened today**.

- **Open today's queue** starts the day for a service.
- **Pause queue** stops new visitors joining and pauses calling. **Resume queue** continues.
- **Close queue** ends the day. It is refused while tickets are still waiting or being served: complete, cancel or transfer them first.

Until a queue is opened, other roles see that a supervisor has not opened it today.

## Who can do this

Supervisor, Facility administrator, Organization administrator.
