# Approve a request

1. In the queue console, find **Requests waiting for approval**.
2. Check the ticket and who asked, then press **Approve**.

You cannot approve a request you made yourself. Requests expire, and an expired or decided request can no longer be approved.

## Switching it off

A facility administrator can turn staff-assisted recovery off for a service. Staff then offer a new ticket instead.

## Who can do this

Supervisor, Facility administrator, Organization administrator.
