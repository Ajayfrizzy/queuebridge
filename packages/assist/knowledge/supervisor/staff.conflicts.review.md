# Conflict review

Open [connectivity and recovery](route:staff.connectivity) and find **Conflict review**. Each card shows the action, the ticket or temporary reference, the device and person, and why the server could not apply it.

1. Choose a **Reason for your decision**.
2. Press **Apply now** to try it against the live queue. If it is still not valid, nothing changes.
3. Or press **Dismiss**.

Earlier decisions stay under **Earlier decisions and refused actions**.

## Who can do this

Supervisor, Facility administrator, Organization administrator.
Auditors can read the list but cannot resolve anything.
