# Recovery activity

A queue's audit trail has a **Recovery activity** section. It lists staff recovery requests, approvals, restricted assistance, tickets opened on a visitor's device, new recovery keys, devices signed out and many failed attempts.

- Each entry shows the ticket, what happened, who did it, the reason and how the visitor was checked.
- No key, code or other secret is recorded.
- Names shown are current display names.

## Who can open it

Supervisor, Facility administrator, Organization administrator, Auditor (read-only). Reception and counter staff cannot open it.
